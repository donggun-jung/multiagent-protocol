# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) without modification. The short version:

- **Treat others with respect.** Disagree on the substance, never on the person.
- **Welcome newcomers.** Assume good faith; help, do not gatekeep.
- **No harassment.** Of any kind, public or private, on any axis.

## Enforcement

Report a violation to the address in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Reports are confidential. Maintainers may warn, ban, or escalate at their discretion. Disputes about enforcement are resolved by majority of active maintainers.

Because this is a best-effort project (`MAINTAINERS.md`), enforcement may be slow. If you need an immediate response, GitHub's own moderation tools (block, report) are usually faster than our reaction.
