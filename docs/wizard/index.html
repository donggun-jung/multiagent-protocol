multiagent-protocol setup wizard

Generate config files for your installation. Nothing is sent anywhere — this runs entirely in your browser.

1 Owner identity

The GitHub login of the person who will approve Quadrant D PRs and push break-glass commits. The bot trusts reactions/comments only from these logins.

2 Supervised repositories

Which repos do you want the bot to gate? One per line, in owner/repo form.

3 Preferences — your working style

How your agents talk to you and how much they decide on their own. This is the personal layer — it goes into your private governance repo and is read by your agents, never by the bot. Everything here has a sensible default; skip fields you do not care about.

Language Language for written deliverables (reports, docs)
Communication Report style How decisions reach you
Autonomy

How much your agents do without asking. This does not change what the bot enforces — Quadrant D always waits for you — it only tunes how your agents behave below that line.

Taste ledger seed (optional)

Working rules you already know you repeat to agents — one per line. Each becomes a dated entry your agents follow from session one. You can keep adding to it later.

Vocabulary seed (optional)

Your shorthand — nicknames, acronyms, internal terms — so agents read your requests like a colleague would. One per line as term: meaning.

4 Runner tier

Where does the bot's cron tick run? Most users start with the Free tier.

5 Built-in skills

Toggle the built-in skills. Leave defaults unless you have a specific reason to disable.

6 Agent registry

Which AI agents do you use? The bot accepts commits only from these agents' identities.

7 Download config files

When you click Generate, the wizard creates the 6 config files. Either download as a ZIP and unzip into your repo, or copy each file individually and open the pre-filled GitHub "create file" link.
